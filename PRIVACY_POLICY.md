Last updated: September 12, 2026

1. Ihya Ulum al-Din Tafsir (`com.ihyatafsir`)
2. RaziApp: Islamic Corpus (`com.ihyatafsir.raziapp`)

## 1. Information Collection and Use
Our applications are designed as educational and spiritual resources. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it:
- No Personal Data Collected: We do not require account registration, and we do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, location, or contacts.
- No Advertising Identifiers: Our applications do not include third-party advertising SDKs or tracking mechanisms that collect advertising IDs.
- Analytics: We do not use intrusive third-party analytics services. Basic crash diagnostics and platform statistics may be processed by Google Play in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

## 2. Device Permissions
Our applications may request minimal permissions necessary for core educational functionality:
- Foreground Service / Media Playback: To ensure uninterrupted audio playback of lectures and Quran recitations when the screen is locked or while using other applications.
- Local Storage / Media Access: To cache audio files, bookmark timestamps, and save study notes locally on your device for offline access. These files never leave your device.
- Internet Access: To stream audio lectures, fetch classical Arabic commentary texts, and check for content updates.

## 3. Data Security
Because our applications do not collect or transmit personal information to external servers, your data remains secure on your local device. Any local bookmarks, reading history, or preferences are stored in private application sandboxes.

## 4. Children's Privacy
Our applications are suitable for all audiences, including families and students of knowledge. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

## 5. Third-Party Services and Links
The applications may contain references or links to scholarly resources and educational websites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party websites you visit.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
